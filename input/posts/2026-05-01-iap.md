fourth try, AI edition

With the rise of "vibe coding", there is a coming avalanche of bespoke web applications being published.
These apps have no know CVEs, since they have not existed till now. So all the practices of managing
security of webapps have been thrown on their head. This demands a new approach to securing applications.

With IAP, your application does not get any traffic unless it has been authenticated and authorized
to visit your app. This reduces the scope of threat you have to defend against from the whole freaking
internet to just insider threats from the people you authroize to use your app.

third try:

Historically the devision between public websites and private websites was drawn using network level
security. The private websites were only accessible if you were plugged into an outlet in the wall
or used a VPN to connect to the network. But with the rise of remote work, the VPN has both become
a bottleneck and not a good enough security boundery.
